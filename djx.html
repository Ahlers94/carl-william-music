    MIDI I/O Management
    SysEx enabled. Connect your Yamaha DJX via USB-MIDI or a 5-pin interface. Physical knob movements on the hardware will sync back to the editor when the DJX transmits CC or XG parameter-change responses.
    MIDI Learn Map
    Click Assign next to any voice, effect, or LFO-eligible parameter, then move a knob or fader on your MIDI controller. The incoming CC is bound to that parameter and will continue to drive it (translated into the correct XG SysEx) whenever it's moved.
      Quick Reference
      XG System On
      F0 43 10 4C 00 00 7E 00 F7

      Part Param Change
      F0 43 10 4C 08 NN AA DD F7

      NN = Part 0–15 (Ch 1–16)
      AA = Address (Cutoff 18h, Res 19h, A/D/R 1A–1Ch)

      Native Voice Select
      Bn 00 mm (Bank MSB)
      Bn 20 ll (Bank LSB)
      Cn pp (Program Change)

      Mixer CCs
      Vol=CC7 · Pan=CC10 · Rev=CC91 · Cho=CC93
